Poems - The Song of Souls

Poems are pieces of writing that talk about a series of topics. Poems are of different types. Some Poems are long while others are short. Some end with rhyming words while others read like monologues.

Poems are a window into the poets soul. The meaning is often clear but somtimes you have to read between the lines to understand the meaning of a poem.

Poems can be interpreted into as many ways as the readers reading them but the true essence lies with the poet itself.

Some renownend Indian poets are Harivansha Rai Bachchan, Ramdhari Singh Dinkar, Rabindranath Tagore and Sarojini Naidu among others.

A fun fact, my grandfather is also a poet, after his retirement, he induldged in poem writing full time and is invited to many Kavi Sammelans.

In my opinion, everyone should write poem sometimes, to let their souls sing in form of words on paper.

Below is a Poem I wrote recently:

What to Learn?

Light finds its way through the cracks of dawn,
Wake up and make the day your own.

Water travels through curves high and low.
Let your mind and body get into flow.

Sound can reach rarest of places around,
Make the most of this life that you found.

Air is filled in the lungs and out,
Choose your words wisely throughout.

Earth takes and gives what it needs,
Keep your mind in creative feeds.

Food is the fuel of body and soul,
Treat with gratitude, your life in whole.

So, treat your life like a pallete and colour it with beauty of poems.
